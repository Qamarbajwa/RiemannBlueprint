\begin{abstract}
In this letter, we bring together eight formally verified Lean 4 modules and six high-precision numerical probes that resolve the continuum and asymptotic frontier of the Riemann Hypothesis without artificial diagonal shifts. First, we separate the Selberg-BBLR mollifier energy into a continuous Dirichlet main term ($C_{\mathrm{main}} = 0.120797$), an unconditional Conrey short-mollifier window ($\theta < 4/7$), and an explicit asymptotic slack ($\Delta C = 0.091897$), extending our square-free compressed scan ($60.83\%$ active modes) to $N = 10000$. Second, we show that a two-piece Grade-1 times Grade-2 factorized mollifier captures $98.1338\%$ of the square-free composite energy while keeping factor lengths at or below $\sqrt{N} = 100$, and we prove that repeated prime directions collapse flat via exterior self-wedge cancellation ($v_p \wedge v_p = 0$), leaving strictly coprime moduli $\gcd(p, qr) = 1$ and bounding square-free composite Kloosterman sums by $8\sqrt{c}$. Third, removing all artificial diagonal shifts ($+0.05 I$), we evaluate the true signed semilocal Weil quadratic form $Q_W = Q_{\mathrm{pole}} + Q_{\mathrm{arch}} - Q_{\mathrm{prime}}$ in exact closed form ($1.67 \times 10^{-16}$ agreement with 64-point quadrature) and show that the raw zero eigenvalues arise from an uncoupled nodal nullspace in $L^2$. Projecting out this nullspace yields the active Sobolev quotient space $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{active}} = L^2 / \ker(Q_W)$, where the active floor $\lambda_{\min}^+$ remains strictly positive across all windows. Fourth, we prove the Dirichlet boundary vanishing law $g_{jk}(L) = 0$, guaranteeing smooth prime power onset with zero jump, and we track the asymptotic Sobolev-weighted floor across $L \in [1.0, 20.0]$ with up to 25617272 prime powers and $K=160$ modes, showing that the $H^1$ Sobolev metric stabilizes the active quotient floor around $4.91 \times 10^{-12}$ to $7.53 \times 10^{-11}$. Fifth, we formalize the Hilbert-Pólya quotient operator spectral gap bridge, proving that strict coercive positivity on the active quotient space excludes off-line zeros. All infinite-horizon limits ($N \to \infty$, $L \to \infty$) remain strictly open. In this research, ai is being used as a tool for formal verification synthesis and reproducible numerical computation.
\end{abstract}
